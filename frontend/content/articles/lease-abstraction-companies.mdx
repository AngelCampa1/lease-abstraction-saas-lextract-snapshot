The lease abstraction market in 2026 has two segments: managed services where human abstractors (often AI-assisted) deliver certified abstracts, and AI software tools where CRE professionals review AI output themselves. The right choice depends on your accuracy requirements, transaction stakes, and internal capacity to validate output.

## What Lease Abstraction Companies Do

Lease abstraction companies extract structured data from commercial lease documents - capturing every material data point (dates, rent schedules, CAM provisions, renewal options, insurance requirements, risk clauses) into a standardized format that CRE professionals can use for due diligence, portfolio management, or loan underwriting.

The value is in compression and structure. A commercial lease runs 60-120 pages of legal language. A lease abstract runs 2-5 pages of structured data containing the same information in a format that can be imported into Yardi, MRI, Airtable, or a financial model. Without abstraction, critical terms - escalation schedules on page 43, renewal notice deadlines buried in exhibits - remain locked in unstructured PDFs until something goes wrong.

Lease abstraction is used in five primary workflows:
- **Acquisition due diligence** - reviewing all leases in a target portfolio before closing
- **Loan underwriting** - satisfying lender requirements for lease data as part of mortgage origination
- **Portfolio onboarding** - converting an inherited or acquired portfolio into structured lease data
- **Lease administration setup** - populating a property management system with data from existing leases
- **Renewal analysis** - extracting critical dates and option terms to support renewal strategy

## Top Lease Abstraction Service Providers

### Realogic
Realogic is a Chicago-based CRE advisory firm that has provided lease abstraction services to institutional investors, lenders, and property managers since 1993. They abstract commercial leases using trained analysts and deliver structured output in client-specified templates. Turnaround: 3-5 business days standard, expedited available. Volume pricing for portfolios of 50+ leases. Best for institutional lenders and REIT due diligence teams.

### RE BackOffice
RE BackOffice (REBO) is a technology-enabled abstraction service that uses AI-assisted workflows with human review. REBO is one of the largest dedicated lease abstraction services in North America, with clients including major REITs, investment managers, and property management firms. They offer standardized templates or custom field sets. Pricing: negotiated by volume, typically $75-$175 per lease for standard commercial leases. Turnaround: 1-3 business days.

### CBRE | Lease Administration
CBRE's lease administration services division offers lease abstraction as part of a broader property management and portfolio management offering. Abstraction typically occurs as part of an outsourced property management engagement rather than as a standalone service. For existing CBRE clients, this provides integrated data flow into their property management platform. For teams not already on CBRE's platform, the abstraction-only engagement is less accessible.

### JLL Lease Administration
JLL provides lease abstraction through its property management services division, similarly bundled within broader property and asset management engagements. JLL's scale and technology platform (including AI-assisted workflows) make it a viable option for large institutional portfolios. Standalone abstraction engagements require direct engagement with their services team.

### Cushman & Wakefield
Cushman & Wakefield's lease administration team provides abstraction services through their property and facilities management division. Similar to CBRE and JLL, abstraction is typically part of a broader engagement rather than a standalone service. Best for existing C&W property management clients.

## AI Lease Abstraction Software vs. Services

For teams with the expertise to review AI output, software tools deliver the same structured fields as managed services at a fraction of the cost and in minutes rather than days.

| Factor | AI Software (Lextract) | Managed Services (REBO, Realogic) |
|--------|----------------------|----------------------------------|
| Cost per lease | $15-$50 | $75-$400 |
| Turnaround | 5-15 minutes | 1-5 business days |
| Human review | You review output | Abstractor certifies output |
| Liability coverage | None | Service provider liability |
| Field coverage | 126 structured fields | Custom templates (varies) |
| Confidence scores | Yes (per-field) | No (human judgment) |
| Best for | Due diligence, PMS import, portfolio review | High-stakes transactions, liability-sensitive deals |

The economics shift significantly at volume. For 10 leases: the cost difference between $15 software and $150 service is $1,300. For 100 leases: the difference is $13,000. For 1,000 leases: the difference is $130,000.

## How Much Do Lease Abstraction Companies Charge?

Lease abstraction pricing varies by service model and volume:

**AI software (self-review):**
- Lextract: $15/lease (5-pack: $13/lease, 10-pack: $12/lease)
- LeaseLens: Free in-browser view, $25/lease to export

**AI-assisted managed services:**
- RE BackOffice: ~$75-$175/lease (volume negotiated)
- Realogic: ~$100-$200/lease (standard commercial)

**Traditional outsourced services (BPO):**
- India/Philippines-based firms: $50-$150/lease
- US-based boutiques: $150-$400/lease

**Enterprise CRE platforms:**
- CBRE, JLL, Cushman & Wakefield: Project-based, negotiated
- Prophia, MRI Contract Intelligence: Annual subscription via sales

For a portfolio of 100 standard commercial leases, total abstraction cost ranges from $1,500 (AI software) to $40,000 (traditional BPO) to $75,000-$200,000 for bundled enterprise services.

## When to Hire a Service vs. Use AI Software

**Use a managed abstraction service when:**
- The transaction stakes are high enough to require professional liability coverage
- You need certified, human-reviewed abstracts for institutional investor or lender deliverables
- Your team lacks the CRE expertise to review AI output efficiently
- You need custom template formatting that matches a client's existing system

**Use AI software when:**
- You need fast, structured data for your own due diligence, PMS import, or financial modeling
- Your team has the lease expertise to review confidence-flagged fields
- Cost efficiency is a priority - especially for portfolios of 10+ leases
- You need output in standard formats (Excel, Word) for system import

For most CRE professionals managing their own portfolios or deals, AI software provides the speed and structure needed at a cost that makes abstraction economically viable for every lease. Managed services remain the right choice for institutional deliverables where the abstract itself is a diligence artifact with liability attached.

## Title writing reference

### Structural patterns that work

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Number + hidden element | "15 Red Flags in Commercial Leases That Manual Review Misses" |
| Without painful alternative | "How to Abstract a Lease Without a Paralegal or a Spreadsheet" |
| Specific outcome | "How This PE Fund Extracted 47 Leases in 2 Days" |
| What-they-miss | "The Lease Clause Your Abstraction Team Missed on Page 47" |
| Diagnosis + implication | "Why Your Lease Abstraction Process Is Costing You $200/Lease" |
| Direct comparison | "AI Lease Abstraction vs. Professional Services: Cost and Accuracy" |

### Title avoid list
- Generic: "Understanding Commercial Lease Terms" (no hook)
- Question-only: "Are You Abstracting Leases Correctly?" (too vague, low click intent)
- Corporate: "Lextract Product Overview" (screams ad, zero organic pull)
- Feature-led: "Lextract's AI Extraction Engine Explained" (nobody searches this)

## Competitor channel landscape (CRE YouTube)

Top commercial real estate YouTube presences focus on **landlord-side and investor content** (property management, acquisitions, returns). Almost nobody creates **lease abstraction content** at depth. This is the whitespace:

- Lease abstraction education: near-zero competition
- NNN lease explainers from the tenant/reviewer perspective: sparse
- AI-powered lease extraction software demos: no direct competitor channel exists
- Red flag detection and confidence scoring content: entirely unoccupied

The SEO opportunity is high because the keyword set is low-competition while being high-commercial-intent. Professionals searching these terms are already deep in a workflow and looking for solutions.

## Algorithm notes

- Primary keyword in the first 3 words of the title (YouTube indexes position-weighted)
- Spoken transcript is heavily indexed, naturally weave "lease abstraction," "126 fields," "confidence scoring," "red flags," "NNN lease" into the script dialogue
- Timestamps/chapters rank independently as Google rich snippets, name them with secondary long-tail keywords, not generic labels like "Part 1"
- Mid-roll CTAs outperform end-only CTAs because significant viewer drop-off happens before the final seconds
- Slingshot Strategy: adjusting thumbnail + title alone (no re-edit) can lift CTR by 50-87% on underperforming videos, test packaging before re-shooting content

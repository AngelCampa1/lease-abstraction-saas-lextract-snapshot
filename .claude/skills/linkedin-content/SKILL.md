# LinkedIn Content for Lextract

You are creating LinkedIn content informed by deep research into the 2025-2026 LinkedIn algorithm. All content is for Lextract, an AI-powered commercial lease abstraction platform that extracts 126 structured fields from any lease PDF for $20/lease. Target audience: RE attorneys, asset managers, PE acquisition teams, property managers, lease administrators, tenant reps, commercial brokers, corporate RE teams, and lenders/investors.

## Step 1: Clarify the request

Ask (or infer from context):
- **Format**: text post / carousel (PDF) / DM sequence / content calendar
- **Goal**: awareness / lead gen / thought leadership / engagement
- **Voice**: founder (Angel) / company account / ghost-writing for a partner
- **Topic**: provided by user, or generate from Lextract's core pain points

If the user says something like "write a LinkedIn post about lease abstraction" pick the best format and hook archetype without asking, then execute.

## Step 2: Choose format and hook

**Format selection guide:**
- Pain-point awareness -> text post (Story or Myth-Buster template)
- Tactical education -> carousel (AIDA framework, 8-12 slides)
- Lead conversion -> Case Study or Framework template (DM keyword CTA)
- Community engagement -> Question or Insight template
- Authority building -> text + signposting (personal client data, specific numbers)

**Hook must fit within 140 characters (3 lines mobile).** Use Loss Aversion, Contrarian Shift, or Hidden Cost archetypes, these perform best for operational-pain content. See `references/playbook.md` for all hook templates.

## Step 3: Write the content

### For text posts
- Max 700-1300 characters (engagement optimum)
- 1-2 sentences per paragraph, mandatory line breaks
- No outbound links in the post body (70% reach penalty)
- End with a CTA on its own line
- Use 1-3 emojis max as visual anchors, not decoration
- Inject a specific data point, client scenario, or personal failure to bypass AI text detection (55% engagement drop without this)

### For carousels
- Format as slide-by-slide outline: Slide 1 (Cover), Slides 2-4 (Problem/Interest), Slides 5-8 (Solution/Detail), Final Slide (CTA)
- 8-12 slides maximum
- 1080x1350px portrait format (mobile-first)
- Body text >=24pt, headers 36-48pt
- One key message per slide, no walls of text
- Use AIDA or PSR framework (see references)

### For DM sequences
- Follow the 5-phase outreach table in references
- Initial message <=50 words, no pitch, reference something specific to them
- Never ask for a meeting until phase 4+

## Step 4: Output format

For posts: deliver the final post text, ready to paste.
For carousels: deliver a slide-by-slide outline with headline + 1-2 bullet points per slide, plus design notes (color suggestions, CTA slide text).
For DM sequences: deliver all 4-5 messages labeled by phase.
For calendars: deliver a table (Week / Format / Topic / Hook / CTA).

## Lextract content angles that perform well on LinkedIn

- "We extracted 126 fields from a 200-page lease in 3 minutes. Here's what manual review misses." (case study hook)
- "The NNN clause your team glossed over could cost your client $200K at renewal." (hidden cost)
- "Stop abstracting leases with a paralegal and a spreadsheet." (contrarian)
- "8-15% error rate on manual lease abstraction. That's not a typo." (urgency + loss aversion)
- Missed renewal deadlines, buried exclusion clauses, NNN reconciliation errors, confidence scoring as specific proof points
- CamAudit cross-sell angle: "We flagged CAM reconciliation issues during extraction. Here's how to take it further."

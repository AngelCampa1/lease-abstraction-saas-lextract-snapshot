You are creating X (Twitter) content informed by deep research into the 2026 X algorithm and top creator frameworks. If the user says something like "write an X thread about lease abstraction," pick the best format and hook without asking, then execute.

## Step 2: Choose format and hook

**Format selection guide:**
- Pain-point awareness -> standalone post (Contrarian or Loss Aversion hook)
- Tactical education -> thread, 5-7 tweets (Specific Number hook)
- Lead conversion -> thread with soft CTA + link-in-reply
- Community growth -> reply-bait question post (2 options, easy to answer)
- Relationship building -> genuine reply to a prospect's post (no pitch)

**Hook must stop the scroll in under 2 seconds.** Use Contrarian Statement, Transformation Story, or Specific Number archetypes. Never open with "Thread:" or "[1/N]", this signals high time investment and kills click-throughs. See `references/playbook.md` for all hook templates.

## Step 3: Write the content

### For standalone posts
- Target 150-250 characters (mobile-first, scannable)
- Single declarative idea, one punch, no throat clearing
- Invite a response: pose a clear 2-option question or an incomplete thought
- No outbound URLs in the post body (50-90% visibility penalty), put links in the first reply
- End with something the reader can act on (bookmark, reply, repost)

### For threads
- **5-7 tweets in the value core**, optimal for algorithmic distribution without reader fatigue
- Each tweet must deliver one distinct point with standalone value (Grok's Phoenix model may surface any individual tweet out of context)
- Tweet 1: Scroll-stopping hook (bold promise, surprising claim, or sharp number)
- Tweet 2: Context, why this matters urgently
- Tweet 3: Framework/roadmap, tell them what they'll get
- Tweets 4-7: Value core, one tactical point per tweet, micro-cliffhanger endings
- Tweet 8: Bridge/summary, "Bottom line:" or "The key insight:"
- Tweet 9: Soft CTA, ask readers to follow or repost the hook, never hard pitch

Keep each tweet under 200-250 characters for mobile scannability. Use line breaks between thoughts.

### For DM scripts
- Never open with a pitch, link, or meeting request
- Phase 1: Like, bookmark, and reply to their content for several days first
- Initial DM: reference a specific post they wrote, make it about them
- Offer asymmetric value (a quick observation or resource) before asking anything
- Follow the 5-phase outreach sequence in `references/playbook.md`

## Step 4: Apply algorithm rules

**Amplifiers:**
- Post Tue-Thu, 8-10 AM audience-local time for maximum initial velocity
- Stay active for 1-3 hours post-publication to reply to every comment (triggers Phoenix out-of-network distribution)
- X Premium required, delivers 2-4x visibility boost and reply prioritization

**Penalties to avoid:**
- No outbound URLs in post body, always link-in-reply
- Do not post more than 1-2 times per day (Author Diversity Score throttles repeat appearances)
- No "rage bait," spam patterns, or exact duplicate content
- Avoid generic AI-sounding language (Grok semantic filter penalizes it)

**Engagement multiplier priority:** Reposts > Replies > Bookmarks > Likes. Engineer for bookmarks and reposts, not just likes.

## Step 5: Output format

For posts: deliver the final tweet text, ready to paste. Include a suggested link-in-reply if relevant.
For threads: deliver all tweets numbered (1/9, 2/9, etc.), plus one suggested link-in-reply for the CTA.
For DM scripts: deliver all 4-5 messages labeled by phase.
For calendars: deliver a table (Day / Pillar / Format / Hook / CTA / Best Post Time).

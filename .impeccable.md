## Design Context

### Users
Commercial real estate professionals (brokers, asset managers, property managers, paralegals). They're busy, detail-oriented, and need to trust the output. Context: reviewing lease abstractions at their desk or on-the-go.

### Brand Personality
Trustworthy, intelligent, efficient. Modern B2B SaaS (Linear/Vercel-style but warmer).

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Tone**: Clean enterprise with warmth — not cold/corporate, not playful
- **Primary**: Teal #0D9488 (oklch 0.568 0.105 184) — signals intelligence without coldness
- **Display font**: Bricolage Grotesque (geometric-warm, distinctive)
- **Body font**: Inter
- **References**: Linear, Vercel, Stripe
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS templates, cyan-on-dark AI aesthetic, glassmorphism

### Button Geometry

Buttons are pills. Use fully rounded button geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`, `rounded-full`, or equivalent) for every button and button-styled CTA, including icon buttons, segmented/toggle controls, and link-buttons. Cards, inputs, dialogs, tags, and content containers may keep their own radii; button-like controls should not use square, `rounded-md`, or `rounded-lg` corners unless the user explicitly asks for that exception.
### Design Principles
1. **Clarity over decoration** — every element earns its place
2. **Trust through precision** — exact data, clear confidence levels, no ambiguity
3. **Warmth in enterprise** — teal palette, rounded but not bubbly, professional but approachable
4. **Accessibility first** — WCAG AA minimum, semantic HTML, keyboard navigation
5. **Distinctive, not generic** — avoid AI slop patterns, break symmetry intentionally

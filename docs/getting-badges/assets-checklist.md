## Product Hunt Gallery

Product Hunt recommends gallery images at 1270x760 and requires 2+ images before the gallery is viewable. Final gallery art has been generated from product screenshots and public sample-report screenshots:

- `docs/getting-badges/assets/producthunt-gallery-01.png`
- `docs/getting-badges/assets/producthunt-gallery-02.png`
- `docs/getting-badges/assets/producthunt-gallery-03.png`
- `docs/getting-badges/assets/producthunt-gallery-04.png`
- `docs/getting-badges/assets/producthunt-thumbnail-240.png`

Recommended gallery sequence:

1. Hero: "Commercial lease abstracts in minutes" over the results screenshot.
2. Upload flow: PDF upload and processing.
3. Output: 126 fields with confidence scores.
4. Red flags: CAM and lease-risk checks.
5. Export: Excel, Word, PDF.

If no video is ready, submit without a YouTube video. Do not upload a private or broken video link.

## Account And Email Checklist

- SaaSHub: use `angel.campa@lextract.io` for domain verification.
- AlternativeTo: create or use a verified personal account.
- Product Hunt: use a personal maker account, not a company account.
- G2: use a company/domain email and request or claim the Lextract product profile.
- BetaList: submit through an authorized founder or company representative account.

## Before Uploading Assets

- Confirm logo is readable at small size.
- Prefer light-mode screenshots unless a platform gallery looks better with mixed screenshots.
- Crop only if the product UI remains clear.
- Do not obscure the product with oversized marketing text.
- Do not submit screenshots that show private lease data, keys, emails, internal staging URLs, or fictional names unless the screenshot is visibly from the public sample/demo report.

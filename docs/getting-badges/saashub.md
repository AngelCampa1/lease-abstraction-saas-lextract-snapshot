## Long Description

Lextract is AI-powered commercial lease abstraction software for commercial real estate teams, brokers, attorneys, property managers, lenders, and investors.

Upload a commercial lease PDF and get a 126-field abstract across parties, key dates, rent, CAM, options, insurance, defaults, and operational clauses. Each field includes a confidence score so reviewers can quickly see what looks clear and what deserves a closer look.

Lextract also checks for 20 lease red flags, including CAM-related issues such as missing audit rights, uncapped charges, and expense language that may need review. Results export to Excel, Word, and PDF.

Pricing is simple: $15 per lease, no subscription, and credits never expire.

## Requirement Notes

- Post from a personal account, not a company account.
- Use a direct product URL, not a blog or press page.
- Product name should be only the name.
- Include a very short tagline, a few strong topics, pricing tag, status, thumbnail, gallery, makers, X account if available, and first comment.
- Thumbnail should be square. Product Hunt recommends 240x240.
- Gallery images should be 1270x760. Product Hunt needs 2+ images before the gallery is viewable.
- Product description is limited to 260 characters.
- YouTube is the supported video format.
- Scheduled launches go live at 12:01 AM PST.
- Do not ask people for upvotes. Ask for feedback.

## First Comment

Hey Product Hunt,

I built Lextract because commercial lease abstraction is still a weirdly manual job. Commercial leases can be long, the important terms are scattered everywhere, and the output often ends up in a spreadsheet that someone has to rebuild from scratch.

Lextract keeps the scope narrow: upload a commercial lease PDF and get a structured abstract back. It extracts 126 fields, scores confidence on each field, flags lease terms worth checking, and exports to Excel, Word, or PDF.

It is not trying to replace legal review. The goal is to make the first pass faster and make human review less painful. Instead of rereading the whole lease to find dates, CAM terms, options, and rent logic, you can start from a structured report and check the fields that need attention.

The pricing is simple: $15 per lease, no subscription.

I would love feedback on two things:

1. Does the positioning make sense if you work around CRE, legal ops, diligence, or property management?
2. What would you need to trust an AI-generated lease abstract in your own workflow?

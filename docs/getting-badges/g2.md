## Requirement Notes

- G2 lists B2B software and services.
- Software companies should have one listing per product.
- Product name must match the actual product name. Use `Lextract`, not a keyword-stuffed name.
- Product website must be accessible when G2 reviews the listing.
- Category eligibility depends on the product's functionality and supporting evidence on the website.
- Free G2 profiles can display logo, description, supported languages, categories, packaging and pricing, features, Q&A, screenshots, seller page, and review collection.
- The "Users Love Us" badge requires 20 reviews with a 4.0+ average.

## Product Description

Lextract helps commercial real estate teams turn commercial lease PDFs into structured lease abstracts without buying a full lease administration platform.

Users upload a lease PDF and Lextract extracts 126 structured fields covering parties, dates, rent, CAM and operating expenses, renewal options, insurance, assignment, defaults, utilities, signage, and other lease terms. Each field includes a confidence score so reviewers can focus on the parts of the abstract that need attention.

Lextract also flags 20 lease issues worth checking, including CAM-related terms such as missing audit rights, uncapped charges, and expense language that deserves a closer look. Results export to Excel, Word, and PDF for handoff into review, diligence, accounting, reporting, or property management workflows.

Pricing is $15 per lease with no subscription. Credit packs are available, and credits never expire.

## Review Qualification Notes

Ask only real users. Do not ask friends, contractors, or contacts who have not used the product. G2 review moderation is stricter than normal directory comments, and weak reviews can waste the first review drive.

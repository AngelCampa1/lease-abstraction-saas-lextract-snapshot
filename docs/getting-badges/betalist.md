## Requirement Notes

- Startup should be new, unreleased, private beta, or recently launched.
- Must be a technology startup.
- Must not be a blog, course, book, newsletter, e-commerce subscription, consultancy, or agency.
- Must have its own domain.
- Free hosting subdomains and app-store-only links are not accepted.
- Landing page should look distinct and explain the product.
- Visitors should be able to sign up, download, log in, or get access.
- Submitter must be authorized to represent the startup.
- Editorial review is not guaranteed. Regular review can take weeks, and accepted submissions may feature later.

## Startup Description

Lextract is an AI tool for commercial lease abstraction. Upload a commercial lease PDF and get a structured lease abstract with 126 fields, confidence scores, red flag checks, and exports to Excel, Word, or PDF.

Commercial leases are dense, and the details that matter are rarely in one place. Lextract is built for the first pass: pull out the parties, dates, rent logic, CAM terms, options, insurance requirements, default language, and operational clauses so a reviewer can start from organized data instead of a blank spreadsheet.

The product is designed for commercial real estate teams, brokers, attorneys, property managers, lenders, and investors. It costs $15 per lease and does not require a subscription.

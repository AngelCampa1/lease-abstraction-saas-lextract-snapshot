# Requirements Matrix

Verified on 2026-05-15.

| Platform | Fit for Lextract | Required or Expected | Assets | Approval Notes | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SaaSHub | Strong. Lextract is SaaS/software, live, English, and on its own domain. | Website URL, categories, competitors, live released product, English site, own domain. Domain email verification helps priority. | Logo or icon, screenshots helpful, competitor list. | All products go through approval. Products not released, waitlist-only pages, free subdomains, and non-English products can be rejected. | Low |
| AlternativeTo | Strong. Best framed as an alternative to manual lease abstraction, outsourced abstracts, and generic AI summary workflows. | Verified AlternativeTo account to suggest or edit apps. App page should include name, icon, concise description, license model, app type, platforms, official links, features, tags, screenshots, alternatives. | Icon, screenshots, optional video link. | AlternativeTo is community-edited. Approval and edits may depend on user trust and moderation. | Medium |
| G2 | Good, but review-dependent. Lextract is B2B software, but category placement will depend on G2's category criteria and website evidence. | One profile per product, B2B product, accessible website, exact product name, feature evidence on the website, category request. | Logo, screenshots, description, pricing, feature list, review collection plan. | A free profile can collect reviews. The "Users Love Us" badge needs 20 reviews with a 4.0+ average. More advanced badges need paid plan access. | Medium |
| BetaList | Conditional. Submit only if Lextract can honestly be documented as unreleased, private beta, or recently launched at submission time. | New or recently launched technology startup, own domain, decent landing page, clear value proposition, way to sign up or access the product. | Logo, screenshot, concise startup description. | Editorial review. Regular review can take weeks, accepted submissions may feature later. Priority speeds review but does not guarantee acceptance. | Medium |
| Product Hunt | Strong if treated as a launch campaign. Weak if submitted without warm audience and product media. | Personal account, direct product URL, product name only, tagline, topics, thumbnail, pricing tag, status, gallery, 260-character description, makers, first comment, scheduled launch. | 240x240 thumbnail, 2+ gallery images at 1270x760, YouTube video optional, interactive demo optional. | Featured placement favors useful, live digital products with novelty, craft, and clear information. Do not ask for upvotes. | High |

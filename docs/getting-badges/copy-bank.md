## Long Description, General

Lextract is AI-powered commercial lease abstraction software for teams that need lease data out of PDFs with typical processing in 5-15 minutes, depending on document length and complexity. Upload a commercial lease PDF and Lextract extracts 126 structured fields across parties, dates, rent, CAM, options, insurance, defaults, and operational clauses.

Each field includes a confidence score, so reviewers can focus on the fields that need attention. Lextract also checks for 20 red flags, including CAM-sensitive terms such as missing audit rights, uncapped charges, and expense language that deserves review.

Reports can be exported to Excel, Word, and PDF. Pricing is simple: $15 per lease, no subscription, and credits never expire.
